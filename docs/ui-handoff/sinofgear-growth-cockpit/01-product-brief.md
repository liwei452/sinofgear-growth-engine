# 01 Product Brief

## Product

SinoFGear is an AI-assisted promotion and customer-acquisition workspace for early-stage export factories. It connects factory/product evidence, market selection, lawful company discovery, opportunity review, content preparation, human-approved social publishing, follow-up, and attribution.

## Primary user

A factory owner or export manager who is commercially experienced but does not want to operate a technical CRM or developer console. The user needs clear next actions, visible evidence, and safe human approval.

## Experience principles

1. Business task first; system configuration second.
2. Evidence before AI claims.
3. One strong action per region.
4. Progressive disclosure instead of long all-at-once pages.
5. Honest empty states instead of fake dashboard data.
6. Human approval before outreach or publication.
7. Chinese-first operating UI with correct English platform/product terms.

## Approved style

- Professional blue and white.
- Calm, compact, evidence-driven industrial SaaS.
- Moderate dashboard density.
- Clear cards and borders, restrained shadows.
- No purple AI gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism, cartoon illustration, emoji navigation, or decorative fake charts.

## Five-second test

On every primary page, a factory owner should immediately understand:

- what is happening;
- what is blocked;
- what evidence exists;
- what the next valid action is.

## Explicit non-goals

- Do not redesign or modify the independent foreign-trade website.
- Do not change backend models, APIs, permissions, AI behavior, OAuth, publishing, discovery, tracking, or CRM behavior.
- Do not add demo customers or invented performance.
- Do not connect real accounts or publish real content.
- Do not add paid APIs, scraping, production deployment, DNS, billing, or infrastructure work.

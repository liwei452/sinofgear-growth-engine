# 03 Page Blueprints

## Shared shell

Desktop uses a fixed left sidebar and compact sticky top bar. Mobile uses an accessible navigation drawer. The sidebar contains only five primary destinations. Company identity, Help, and Settings sit at the bottom with lower visual emphasis.

Top bar content is compact: current page title, optional one-line context, real notifications only, and user menu. Remove redundant location labels.

## Page 1: Today / 今天

Desktop composition follows `reference-dashboard.png`:

- Left, about 58%: verified opportunity queue.
- Right, about 42%: AI visibility/readiness above channel performance.

Opportunity item fields:

- company and country;
- verified industry/size when available;
- need summary;
- source and observed time;
- evidence-backed intent state;
- one primary action: add to follow-up or the next valid stage.

Open evidence, public contact routes, draft generation, and follow-up history in a right detail drawer. Do not expand every item into a long card.

If AI visibility observations or metrics do not exist, keep the panel footprint but show a readiness checklist and one next action. Never render a decorative score or zero trend.

## Page 2: Promotion / 推广

Replace the long page with a four-step flow:

1. Select market.
2. Prepare content.
3. Connect accounts.
4. Review and publish.

Show current step, progress, market/ICP summary, provider/cost warning when real, and one next action. Expand only the current step. Collapse completed steps into concise summaries. Blocked steps state exactly what prerequisite is missing.

Keep Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube. Keep AI recommendation, generated draft, verified facts, account readiness, manual export, content review, publishing results, and failure recovery. Move detail into step panels or drawers; do not delete it.

## Page 3: Customer Opportunities / 客户机会

Desktop master-detail workspace:

- Left: searchable/filterable list.
- Right: selected opportunity details.

List shows company, country, need, source, confidence/evidence state, and follow-up stage. Detail shows evidence and score explanation, company profile, public contact path, enrichment status, activity timeline, and valid actions.

On mobile, list and detail are separate URL/back-stack states. Preserve filters, sort, selection, and scroll when returning.

## Page 4: Results / 效果

Three levels:

1. Recorded KPI summary.
2. Conversion path from discovery to inquiry.
3. Channel/account attribution records.

Use horizontal bars for channel comparison, line charts only with at least four time points, and a linear funnel with visible counts and conversion percentages. Every chart has a table/text alternative. If data is insufficient, state the missing input and link to the relevant task.

## Page 5: My Company / 我的公司

This is the source-of-truth readiness page, not a settings dump.

Sections:

- confirmed company facts and facts awaiting review;
- products and verified product facts;
- uploaded assets/documents and understanding status;
- content readiness and gaps;
- social account readiness summary.

Completeness must be derived from real records and named prerequisites. Do not show an arbitrary percentage. Link contextual actions to upload, product editing, fact verification, knowledge management, and the Promotion connection step.

## Secondary routes

Keep existing routes for products, knowledge, content factory, review center, assets, publishing calendar, platform accounts, settings, and advanced analytics. They remain contextual destinations and must not become extra primary sidebar items.
